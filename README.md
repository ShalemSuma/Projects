# Research Projects

This repository contains completed research projects and data exercises in economics and related fields. Each folder includes assignments, datasets, or research papers on various topics. All projects were done either as coursework or with faculty at NYU.

## Projects Overview

- **Econometrics Data Exercises**: Assignments covering econometric analysis and data exercises.
- **Effect of Educational Programs & Tax Relief on Labour Market Outcomes**: Research on how education and tax policies impact labor markets.
- **Female Labour Force Participation**: Analysis of factors influencing women's participation in the workforce.
- **Forecast of E-Commerce Retail Sales**: Study forecasting trends in online retail sales.
- **Interest Heterogeneity in Network Formation on Large Online Platforms**: Examination of how differing interests affect network formation online.
- **Macroeconomics Data Exercises**: Assignments and exercises in macroeconomic data analysis.
- **The Effect of Familiarity on Facial Positivity**: Research on psychological effects of familiarity in facial recognition.
- **The Effect of Political Freedom on Life Expectancy**: Study of the relationship between political freedom and life expectancy.
- **The Effects of Minimum Wage on Unemployment**: Analysis of minimum wage policies and their impact on unemployment rates.

## Technical Skills Utilized

Projects utilize Python (with libraries such as pandas, numpy, matplotlib, scikit-learn, and statsmodels), R, Stata, Jupyter Notebooks, and Excel for data analysis, statistical modeling, and visualization.

Each directory contains relevant documentation, assignments, or research papers
